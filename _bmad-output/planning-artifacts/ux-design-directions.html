Three design directions for PodDrive

Each direction applies the same colour tokens and experience principles to the two key screens: the Drive Session (the core experience) and the Idle/Management screen (podcast library and tier setup). Click a direction tab to explore the full mockups.

Direction 1

Minimal Focus

A single central card owns the drive session. Maximum whitespace, episode count as the hero element. Everything else steps back.

Voice-first High contrast Minimal UI Low density
Direction 2

Dashboard

A structured two-zone layout: briefing status at top, scrollable episode queue below. More information visible at a glance.

Queue visible Structured List-based Medium density
Direction 3

Ambient

A warm, atmospheric treatment with subtle glows and a frosted card. Evokes Headspace's calm. The screen feels alive without demanding attention.

Atmospheric Warm Glassmorphism Calm

Direction Comparison

Direction 1 — Minimal Focus

  • Best for: users who glance briefly and rely primarily on voice
  • Drive mode feel: clean, confident, nothing to read
  • Info density: very low — only what matters
  • Risk: may feel too sparse for users who like to see their queue

Direction 2 — Dashboard

  • Best for: users who want to see upcoming episodes before choosing
  • Drive mode feel: structured, in control, information-rich
  • Info density: medium — more context available
  • Risk: more visual complexity; verify it stays glanceable

Direction 3 — Ambient

  • Best for: users who value emotional tone and warmth over data
  • Drive mode feel: calm, atmospheric, Headspace-like
  • Info density: low — visual warmth over information
  • Risk: glow effects must not create distraction; needs careful implementation

Direction 1 — Minimal Focus

The episode count is the hero. A single focused card owns the drive session screen. Maximum whitespace, minimal text, voice is clearly the primary interaction. Inspired by Android Auto's discipline of showing only what's essential.
